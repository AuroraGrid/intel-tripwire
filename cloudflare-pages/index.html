Operational capabilities

The beta combines source collection, provenance, verification, geographic context, operational health, and analyst workflows in one environment.

01

Global operating picture

Monitor incidents, transport, infrastructure, markets, and source health from a unified interface.

What friends are testing

The objective is not promotional approval. It is finding defects, unclear workflows, weak alerts, missing context, and incorrect assumptions.

Private beta brief

Testers receive separate access credentials and evaluate the product as an operational tool. The beta remains a work in progress: individual sources can degrade, coverage varies by domain, and every assessment should retain explicit uncertainty.

Access instructions are sent directly by the operator. Do not forward credentials, API tokens, screenshots containing sensitive data, or internal beta links.

Tester rules

  • ✓Report broken pages, stale data, and misleading status indicators.
  • ✓Flag false positives and important events the system missed.
  • ✓Distinguish verified facts from interpretation in feedback.
  • ✓Do not use the beta as the sole basis for safety-critical decisions.
